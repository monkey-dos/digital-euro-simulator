# Banking app

**Run by:** bank customers

**Does:** Open account, register phone alias, load/unload money, pay by alias or DEAN, balance, history.

**Offers interfaces:** –

**Uses interfaces:** I1 (only its own bank)

Messages per ECB Annex D1-1; screens are our own design. Several independent apps are welcome: any bank may pick any app that speaks I1.

Status: not started. Build against the contracts in `/contracts`.
