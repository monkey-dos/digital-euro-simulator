# ADR 0003: Everything is visible in the observer

Status: accepted (1 November 2026)

## Decision
The transparency observer shows every message and every balance in full, including encrypted parts (decrypted for display).

## Why
All participants are fictional; the purpose is understanding. Slow motion (configurable delay per hop) replaces real-time SLAs.

## Consequences
Optional lenses show what each party may actually see (DESP, a bank, the public), so the privacy design stays visible.
